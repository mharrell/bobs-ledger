# Demons — Shop Buff

> Extracted from Shadybunny's commentary on Beater's tier-7 game
> (video `BPG_wv3q1E0`). Card names normalized from auto-captions.

## The engine

The comp **buff the shop**, then **eat the huge shop** for massive stats. Two
layers:

1. **Shop buffs.** Demons that buff the shop (the comp's core). "Shop buffs are
   also good." The shop grows, and everything you buy comes in big.
2. **Sargeras** — buffs the whole shop. "The advantage of playing Sargeras and
   buffing your whole shop is that you have very easy pivot stuff… when your
   whole shop is big, you can just buy a pirate cleave and it'll have 100/100."
3. **The payoff: eat the shop.** Make one demon as big as possible, then
   **Avalanche** it (or Felboar) to convert the huge shop into board stats. "It's
   just all about making a big demon as big as possible and an avalanche."

## Key cards

| Card | Role |
|------|------|
| Sargeras | Buffs the whole shop — the engine's amplifier. |
| Shop-buff demons | The core — scale the shop. |
| Avalanche / Felboar | The payoff — eat the huge shop for stats. |
| Titus | Doubles deathrattles (for the Stitch/Cotto line). |

## High-tier tech: Stitch + Cotto

A common high-tier combo that pairs with this: **Stitch + Cotto + Titus** (an
Undead deathrattle engine). "Once you have stitch plus cotto, it's such an easy
way to get bigger stats… the cotto is times two and then all the other cotto also
get added to that latest cotto." It's a strong alternative/tech when the lobby
goes Undead.

## Traps / anti-patterns

- **Don't over-buff at the cost of the fight.** "The game is about not losing…
  you want to buff, but it's not just a stat building simulator. You want to also
  win your fights." A shop-buff that costs you a 5k/5k on the board is a bad
  trade.
- **Stitch/Cotto is hard to beat once it's running** — it spawns too many bodies
  for Leroy/cleave to handle. "Stitch Cotto has way too many bodies."
- **Start-of-turn ordering matters** — a full board can make your start-of-turn
  effects fire first, which decides Stitch-vs-Stitch mirrors.

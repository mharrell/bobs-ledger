# Mechs — Divine Shield

> Our advice for the published B-tier build (hsreplay tier list, 2026-11-16).
> Written as original advice for this coach; card texts read from the DB.

## The engine

A **shield-payoff engine**: cheap Tavern spells become Attack, but only on
minions that still have a Divine Shield, and the shield leaving is itself a
buff trigger.

1. **Charging Czarina** (t5) — Divine Shield. Whenever you cast a Tavern
   spell, give your minions with Divine Shield +4 Attack. The payoff, and the
   reason the board must stay shielded.
2. **Gearfin** (t4, Mech/Murloc) — at the end of your turn, get two 1-Cost
   Tavern spells. The spell printer that feeds Czarina every single turn.
3. **Hopebringer** (t5, Dragon) — Start of Combat: give your minions +3/+3,
   and the amount permanently improves after a friendly minion loses Divine
   Shield. The long-game scaler.
4. **Falling Sky Golem** (t6, Mech) — Divine Shield, and +4/+2 for each
   Deathrattle you have triggered this game. The shielded body that becomes a
   threat on its own.

## The loop

- Put Czarina down first, then keep two cheap spells flowing every turn from
  Gearfin. Each cast is +4 Attack to every shielded minion — a wide shielded
  board turns one spell into four or five stat lines.
- Hopebringer pays the whole board +3/+3 at the start of combat and improves
  whenever a shield pops. The comp *wants* fights that break shields; that is
  how the permanent buff grows.
- Falling Sky Golem is the Deathrattle-count payoff. Any Deathrattle you
  trigger this game — from your board, your trinkets, or the shop — makes it
  bigger, so it is a reason to keep one Deathrattle piece around.

## Traps / anti-patterns

- **Czarina is 4/1.** It dies to almost anything. Give it the shield first,
  position it behind taunts, and do not cast your spells before it is on the
  board — an unscaled spell is just a spell.
- **The payoff is "with Divine Shield", not "Mech".** A Mech without a shield
  gets nothing from Czarina; a shielded non-Mech does. Shield first, tribe
  second.
- **Hopebringer's improvement needs the shield to break.** Avoiding all
  trades to keep shields up also keeps the permanent buff small; the comp
  trades, it does not sit.
- **Cheap spells, not big ones.** Gearfin prints 1-Cost spells; the engine is
  volume, so spend the two every turn rather than saving for a big cast.

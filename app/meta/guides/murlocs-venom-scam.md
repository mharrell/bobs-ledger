# Murlocs — Venom Scam

> Extracted from Shadybunny's commentary on a Brann game
> (video `zxCxDntt5ZU`). Card names normalized from auto-captions.

## The engine

The comp is a **battlecry engine** (Brann + Rylak) feeding **Morgl**, with
**Chameleon** copying it and **venom/poison** as the scam payoff.

1. **Brann** — doubles battlecries.
2. **Rylak** — triggers battlecries.
3. **Morgl** — the payoff (scales with battlecries).
4. **Chameleon** — copies a minion's stats/keywords. "I can use Carl to buff
   your Margo and then copy Margo."
5. **Venom/poison** — the scam. "Got a venom left, it's over."

## The loop

- Play battlecries (Brann + Rylak double/trigger them) → Morgl scales.
- Chameleon copies Morgl (or a buffed minion) to multiply.
- Add venom/poison to close — "Got a venom left, it's over."

## Traps / anti-patterns

- **Don't add extra types** — "I didn't want to add another type to the board.
   I don't want to open up ogre trinkets." Keep the board focused.
- **Positioning matters** — put the buff target (Carl/Morgl) first so Chameleon
  copies the right minion.
- **The comp is APM-heavy** — "I cannot think about it cuz it will take too
  long." Manage hand/board space.

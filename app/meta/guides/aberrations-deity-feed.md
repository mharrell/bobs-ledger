# Aberrations — Deity Feed

> Player-curated build (2026-09-24), refined against our own replay corpus.
> This is our advice, written for this coach — the companion to the published
> "Deathrattle/Spells" build, which shares half its core.

## The engine

Two loops that pay the same bank: the **Deity pool** and **end-of-turn spell
scaling**. Tavern spells are the fuel for both.

1. **Felfire Conjurer** (t5) — every tavern spell gives an extra +1/+1 for the
   rest of the game. The floor-raiser: hold it early, every later spell is
   bigger.
2. **Drakkari Enchanter** (t5) — end-of-turn effects fire twice. Doubles both
   Felfire Conjurer's scaling and Mysterious K'Thir's payoff.
3. **Mysterious K'Thir** (t5) — at end of turn discards your 3 left-most
   tavern spells and gains +8/+8 per spell.
4. **N'raqi Sapper** (t5) — battlecry AND deathrattle: get an Energizing
   Chamber (+7/+7 Deity; if discarded, casts twice).
5. **Faceless Converter** (t5) — deathrattle: Deity +1/+1, improved by each
   tavern spell you've cast all game.
6. **Titus Rivendare** (t5) — deathrattles trigger twice.

## How it plays

- **Turn loop** (Drakkari-doubled): Felfire lifts every spell → K'Thir eats
  your three left-most hand spells at end of turn → any Energizing Chamber in
  that discard casts twice into the Deity pool.
- **Death loop** (Titus-doubled): your Aberrations die, advancing the
  3-sacrifice meter; their deathrattles bank Deity stats. The third
  sacrifice awakens the Deity in combat — C'Thun splits the banked pool
  across your board; Y'Shaarj re-summons two dead Aberrations instead.
- **Curve**: Drifting Sacrifice (t3, Reborn deathrattle Deity feed) opens;
  Mindbending Recruiter and Nightmare Corroder (t4) hold the direction; the
  t5 wall is the commit.

## Traps / anti-patterns

- **Don't buy scaling while dying.** The whole build is t4/t5 payoffs; if the
  lobby is pounding you, buy stat bodies instead of the next engine piece.
- **Selling the death-feed half is fine.** In practice the spell-scaling half
  (Conjurer/K'Thir/Drakkari) does the work; Converter/Corroder/Frostcaller
  are the first cuts when the board is tight.
- **A golden does not complete a triple** — regular copies only.
- **Mind Muck is a demon tool, not aberration glue** — it needs a friendly
  Demon target, so it doesn't fit this board.

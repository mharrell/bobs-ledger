# Mechs — Magnetics

> Extracted from Shadybunny's commentary on Zorgo's game
> (video `Tsr5xfReDlY`). Card names normalized from auto-captions.

## The engine

The comp is built around **Spark Snapper** — every Mech you play gets a
Magnetic Satellite, and the Snapper improves. The real power is **stacking**:
magnetize everything onto one Mech, pick it back up (Repeat Customer / a triple),
and re-play it so the stack grows bigger and bigger.

1. **Spark Snapper** — the core. "The snappers make it so that every time you
   play a mech the mech gets the buffs again."
2. **Stacking** — magnetize all onto one Mech, pick it up, re-play → the stack
   compounds. "He can make the stack bigger and bigger and bigger because of the
   snappers."
3. **Utility Drone** — +stats per magnetization (end-of-turn), the amplifier.
4. **Beatboxer** (tier-7 prize) — copies the stacked stats. The payoff.

## The stacking loop

- Magnetize everything onto one Mech (the "stack").
- Pick it up (Repeat Customer or a triple) and re-play it → the Snapper re-buffs
  it, so the stack grows.
- Triple the stack to double it again, then Beatboxer copies it.
- "The smallest Spark Snapper is the best" — the first Snapper (improving longest)
  is the one to stack on.

## Traps / anti-patterns

- **Don't spread the stats.** Against big opponents, spreading makes a few
  2k/2k units that are pointless vs a 10k unit. Keep the stack concentrated.
- **Don't play pirates without Hooktusk.** "Unless you have Hooktusk, you're not
  playing pirates. You should play whatever tempo you have until you have
  Hooktusk." (A general tempo principle.)
- **Positioning matters** — Beatboxer in the back, the stacked Mech in front.

# Mechs — APM Magnetic

> Our advice for the published A-tier build (hsreplay tier list, 2026-09-30).
> Written as original advice for this coach; card texts read from the DB.

## The engine

An **APM chain engine**: buy a lot, magnetize a lot, and every chain prints
more mechs.

1. **Auto Reveille** (t6) — after you buy 3 cards, get a random Magnetic
   Volumizer (3 left). Pure APM payoff: the more you buy, the more it mints.
2. **Spark Snapper** (t5) — whenever you play a Mech, magnetize a 2/2
   Satellite to it and improve this. Your play-outs grow the engine.
3. **Polarizing Beatboxer** (t7) — whenever you magnetize to a different
   minion, it also magnetizes to this. Every attach doubles onto the box.
4. **Drone Duplicator** (t4) — Divine Shield; Activate (1): the next
   Magnetization this turn happens twice.
5. **Sky Admiral Rogers** (t6) + **Proud Privateer** (t5) — the Bounty pair:
   spend 9 gold → a Bounty, cast twice.
6. **Addons**: Enterprising Escapee (Lockboxes), Brann (battlecries twice).

## The loop

- Buy everything affordable → Reveille's counter fires → play your mechs →
  Spark Snapper magnetizes and improves → Beatboxer mirrors attachments →
  Drone Duplicator's Activate doubles the big one.

## Traps / anti-patterns

- **Magnetize order is real**: Drone Duplicator's Activate before the
  biggest attach; Beatboxer wants to be the LAST magnetize target so it
  mirrors everything.
- **Reveille counts BUYS, not rolls** — rolling three times does nothing for
  it; three cheap buys do.
- **t7 means Beatboxer needs a tier skip or event access** — don't plan the
  midgame around it.

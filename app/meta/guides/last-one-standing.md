# Last One Standing

> Our advice for the published S-tier build (hsreplay tier list, 2026-11-16).
> Written as original advice for this coach; card texts read from the DB.

## The engine

A **menagerie Rally payoff**: one minion of every type on the board, and a
tier-7 All-type body that pays each of them for every Rally it fires.

1. **The Last One Standing** (t7) — Rally: give a friendly minion of each type
   +10/+10 permanently, and the amount improves with every trigger. It is the
   whole comp; nothing else does this.
2. **Sky-hatch Runaway** (t4, Dragon) — Activate (1): trigger a friendly
   minion's Rally. This is the second trigger, bought for one gold, every turn.
3. **Lurking Lionfish** (t2, Beast) — Activate (2): replace a Tavern card with
   a Fishbait for your left-most Beast to attack. The Beast slot.
4. **Elemental of Surprise** (t6, Elemental) — an 8/8 Divine Shield that
   triples with any Elemental. The Elemental slot, and a body that survives.
5. **Blade Collector** (t4, Pirate) — cleave. The Pirate slot.
6. **Greedy Conniver** (t3, Pirate) — if Golden when sold, Discover a Tier 7
   minion: the realistic way to find The Last One Standing. The source's own
   note is not to force this comp, and it is right — without the tier-7 body
   you are playing a pile of one-of-each.

## The loop

- Hold one minion of each type the lobby offers (up to five). The Rally buffs
  **one minion per type**, so a five-type board takes five +10/+10s and a
  one-type board takes one.
- Every turn, fire the Rally again — Sky-hatch Runaway's Activate is the cheap
  one — because the buff also *improves*, so each trigger beats the last.
- The Last One Standing also buffs on its own attack in combat, so position it
  to attack early and often.

## Traps / anti-patterns

- **Do not force it.** It needs a tier-7 minion and five types on board; a
  Golden Greedy Conniver is the only reliable find. If the tier-7 body never
  arrives, this is not the comp.
- **Duplicates are dead weight.** Two Beasts still get one Beast buff. Every
  slot should be a different type until all five are covered.
- **The Activate budget is real:** Sky-hatch 1 gold plus Lionfish 2 gold per
  turn is gold not spent on bodies. Activate the Rally every turn; treat the
  Lionfish as optional once the Beast slot has a body.
- **It is a late-game board.** The pieces are tier 4-7; do not level past a
  board you cannot defend while hunting them.

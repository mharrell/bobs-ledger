# Elementals — Living Azerite

> Our advice for the published A-tier build (hsreplay tier list, 2026-09-30).
> Written as original advice for this coach; card texts read from the DB.

## The engine

A **shop-buff scaler**: spells you cast grow the elementals still sitting in
the Tavern, so your next buys are pre-buffed.

1. **Living Azerite** (t5) — whenever you cast a tavern spell, give
   Elementals in the Tavern +3/+2 this game. Permanent spell-quality growth.
2. **Leyline Surfacer** (t4) — battlecry AND deathrattle: get an Arcane
   Absorption. Spell generation on both ends.
3. **Balinda Stonehearth** (t6) — friendly-targeted spells cast twice.
4. **Brann Bronzebeard** (t5) — battlecries twice.
5. **Kelp Keeper** (t4) — Activate (1): trigger a friendly minion's
   battlecry.
6. **Addons**: Sky Admiral Rogers (spend-gold Bounties), Proud Privateer
   (Bounties cast twice), Wildfire Elemental (combat splasher).

## The loop

- Cast spells (Balinda doubles the targeted ones) → Living Azerite pumps
  every elemental in the shop → buy the buffed bodies → repeat; every spell
  you cast this game makes every future shop richer.

## Traps / anti-patterns

- **The buff lives in the shop, not your hand** — buffed tavern elementals
  vanish on refresh; buy before you roll.
- **Spell-count is the throttle**: Leyline Surfacer's two Arcane
  Absorptions and cheap targeted spells keep Living Azerite ticking.
- **Rogers wants gold SPENT** — same APM note as the other Bounty builds.

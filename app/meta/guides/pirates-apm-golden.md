# Pirates — APM Golden

> Extracted from Shadybunny's commentary on Zorgo's game
> (video `DqPYeFZch0c`). Card names normalized from auto-captions.

## The engine

The comp is built around **Hooktusk** (the first Pirate you buy each turn is
free) plus the **Dagger** trinket, which continuously copies your board and
gives it dark gifts. Stack **Admiration** (Start of Combat: gain the Attack of
the minion to the left) and make it permanent with **Poet**.

1. **Hooktusk** — the key Pirate. "Unless you have Hooktusk, you're not playing
   pirates. You should play whatever tempo you have until you have Hooktusk."
2. **Dagger** (trinket) — continuously copies your board and gives it dark
   gifts. "Dagger is continuously copy your board and give it dark gifts."
3. **Escapee** — generates prizes (the economy engine).
4. **Admiration** (dark gift) — stacking attack. "Admiration into admiration…
   it's like immortal stacking."
5. **Poet** — makes dark gifts permanent.
6. **Brann** — doubles battlecries (for the discovery/value).

## The stacking loop

- Farm **Admiration** on an Escapee (or Brann) via the Dagger.
- Stack multiple Admirations so the minion gains the left minion's attack,
  which cascades ("it's like immortal stacking").
- Make it permanent with **Poet**.
- Position the chain correctly: Brann far left, the doubler next to it, then
  the other Admiration — so the attack cascades down the chain.

## Traps / anti-patterns

- **Don't play pirates without Hooktusk.** "You are not playing pirates when you
  do not have Hooktusk." Play tempo until you hit it.
- **Positioning is everything** — the Admiration chain must be ordered right or
  you copy 7k instead of 120k. "The chain reaction is gone."
- **Don't buy trash** (Jeep rule #1) — skip bad shops rather than fill the board.

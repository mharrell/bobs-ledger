# Demons — APM Shop Buff

> Extracted from Shadybunny's commentary on a Morchie game
> (video `gCXEqUI0nQU`). Card names normalized from auto-captions.

## The engine

The comp **buff the shop** with spells, then **Felboar** converts the spell-casting
into stats. **Belinda** doubles the spells, and **spell power** boosts them.

1. **Felboar** — the payoff. Cast a spell → gain stats. "Golden Felboar… I have
   spell power. I can buff the shop."
2. **Shop buffs** — the core. "Shop buffs are incredibly valuable."
3. **Belinda** — doubles spells (double-casts gems/spells). "Belinda works with
   seafloor and with the felbor. I get double cast."
4. **Spell power** — boosts the buffs.
5. **Seafloor Recruiter** — generates spells.

## The loop

- Generate spells (Seafloor Recruiter), cast them to buff the shop.
- Belinda double-casts; spell power boosts.
- Felboar converts the spell-casting into stats.
- "I just try to generate as many gem barrages as possible."

## Traps / anti-patterns

- **Commit to Felboar** — "I have to commit, threat, so I'm not going to do it."
  Don't half-commit.
- **Board space** — the comp generates a lot; manage hand/board space or you
  lose the Felboar value.
- **Belinda is the key amplifier** — "Belinda is really good to beat the
  Felboar." Protect it.

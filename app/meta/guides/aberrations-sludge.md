# Aberrations — Sludge

> Our advice for the published A-tier build (hsreplay tier list, 2026-09-30).
> Written as original advice for this coach; card texts read from the DB.

## The engine

The **spell-scaling half of the aberration family**, plus a deathrattle that
makes every future spell bigger permanently.

1. **N'raqi Frostcaller** (t5) — Activate (0): discard a card; your tavern
   spells give an extra +1/+1 this game. Free to press — press it every turn.
2. **Mysterious K'Thir** (t5) — end of turn: discards your 3 left-most tavern
   spells, +8/+8 each.
3. **Nightmare Corroder** (t4) — deathrattle: get a Sludge Corrosion (board
   +1/+1; if discarded, casts twice).
4. **Titus Rivendare** (t5) — deathrattles twice: two Corrosions, double
   Dark Puppeteer banks.
5. **Dark Puppeteer** (t6) — deathrattle: your tavern spells give an extra
   +4 HEALTH this game. A permanent spell-quality raise, on death.
6. **Addons**: Felfire Conjurer, N'raqi Sapper (Chambers), Mindbending
   Recruiter (discard fuel + hand-adds), Drakkari Enchanter (EoT twice).

## The loop

- Frostcaller's Activate and bought spells raise spell quality → K'Thir
  converts your left-most hand spells into +8/+8 bodies (doubled by
  Drakkari) → discarded Corrosions cast twice onto the whole board →
  Corroder/Puppeteer deaths (doubled by Titus) raise quality permanently.

## Traps / anti-patterns

- **Frostcaller's Activate is FREE** — there is no reason to end a turn
  without pressing it.
- **K'Thir eats your left-most spells**: order your hand so the spells you
  want converted are left-most and the ones you're saving are right-most.
- **This build overlaps Deity Feed heavily** (Frostcaller/K'Thir/Corroder/
  Sapper/Conjurer/Drakkari) — the divergence is Puppeteer's permanent
  health scaling versus Deity Feed's Converter/Sha bank.

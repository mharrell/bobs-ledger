# Elementals — Ultraviolet

> Our advice for the published B-tier build (hsreplay tier list, 2026-11-16).
> Written as original advice for this coach; card texts read from the DB.

## The engine

A **play-count scaler**: every Elemental you play permanently improves the
Start-of-Combat buff that Ultraviolet Ascendant gives the rest of the board.

1. **Ultraviolet Ascendant** (t6) — Start of Combat: give your OTHER
   Elementals +4/+4, and the amount improves each time you play an Elemental.
   It is the payoff and it does not buff itself.
2. **Tavern Tempest** (t4) — Battlecry: get a random Elemental. A body and a
   play in one card.
3. **Brann Bronzebeard** (t5) — battlecries twice: two Elementals per Tempest.
4. **Kelp Keeper** (t4) — Activate (1): trigger a friendly Battlecry, another
   Tempest in practice.
5. **Air Revenant** (t5) — after you spend 7 Gold, cast Easterly Winds: a
   random Tavern minion gets +8/+8, which also fattens the shop Unbound
   Tempest reads.
6. **Unbound Tempest** (t6) — after you play 3 Elementals, gain the stats of
   the highest-Health minion in the Tavern. The payoff that turns Elemental
   plays into stats, not a body to hold.
7. **Wildfire Elemental** (t3) — the combat splasher; the body you can buy
   while the engine comes online.

## The loop

- Play Elementals every turn: Tavern Tempest bodies, cheap Elemental bodies
  from the shop, and whatever the engine generates. Each play raises
  Ultraviolet's number for the rest of the game.
- Keep the board wide and Elemental-heavy: the buff hits **other** Elementals,
  so it scales with bodies, not with one tall minion.
- Brann plus Kelp Keeper is the engine multiplier: more Tempest battlecries,
  more Elementals, more plays, a bigger buff.

## Traps / anti-patterns

- **Buying is not playing.** The improvement triggers on PLAY. A hand full of
  Elementals is a buff you have not collected yet.
- **Ultraviolet does not buff itself.** Do not treat it as the carry; the
  carry is the wide Elemental board it pumps.
- **Start of Combat is after the shop.** The stats land when the fight starts,
  so a board that is already dead on the shop screen never gets them.
- **Unbound Tempest wants a fat Tavern.** Its payoff reads the highest-Health
  minion in the shop, so a buffed shop (Air Revenant, Tavern spells) is what
  makes it worth the slot.

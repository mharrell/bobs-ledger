# Undead — Attack Scaling

> Extracted from Shadybunny's commentary on Matsuri's game
> (video `LuyDQHg9nmM`). Card names normalized from auto-captions.

## The engine

The comp **farms damage** on Undead, then **Butchering** converts it into stats.
Belinda + spell power double the Butchering procs.

1. **Undead damage farming** — Undead that add damage via deathrattle (the
   "caretaker" minions). "Getting the plus two damage on all the tokens is quite
   nice."
2. **Butchering** — the payoff. Kill off minions to gain their stats. "He's
   going into butcher… it's just all about making a big demon as big as possible
   and an avalanche" (the Butchering line).
3. **Belinda + spell power** — Belinda doubles the Butchering spell procs, and
   spell power boosts them. "The Belinda was a great addition… getting the double
   proc on the butchering making butchering scale with spell power."
4. **Reborn** — Undead come back (the engine's sustain). Double Reborn is strong.

## Late-game transition: Kel'Thuzad + poison/venom

When stats can't win (a big demon/pirate opponent), pivot to a **poison/venom**
board: Reborn a poison minion, put it next to **Kel'Thuzad** to print copies.
"Reborn the venom and then put it next to Kel'Thuzad, you print a copy of it."
This is a dirty, high-risk transition — only do it if you'll lose on stats.

## Traps / anti-patterns

- **Don't over-commit to a transition you can't afford.** "You are introducing
  risk all of a sudden… you have to play down a unit, you have to wait a little
  bit longer." Only pivot to poison if you genuinely can't win on stats.
- **Cleave is the natural enemy of token builds** — but low-stats tokens are
  fine.
- **Hand-space economy** — the comp generates a lot of cards; manage hand space
  or you overflow.

# Nagas — End Of Turn / Spell Buff

> Extracted from Shadybunny's commentary on Beater's game
> ("Double Reno Hero Power?!", video `NhR6GX5lpJQ`, 2026-08-17).
> Card names normalized from auto-captions; quotes are lightly cleaned.

## The engine

The comp scales by **casting targeted spells on a Naga**, which buffs the whole
board. Two distinct engines feed it, and the best games run both:

1. **Spell-power → Fauna Whisperer.** Felfire Conjurer raises spell power;
   Fauna Whisperer turns that spell power into end-of-turn board buffs, doubled
   by Drakkari Enchanter. Best target is Gatekeeper Amalgam (all-tribe, so it
   buffs every tribed unit).
2. **Balinda economy → Torrential Ruiner.** Golden Balinda Stonehearth makes
   every gem/spell cast refund gold, so you have effectively infinite gold to
   keep casting. Torrential Ruiner is the payoff: cast any spell on it and it
   buffs your whole board.

## Commit conditions

- **Golden Balinda is economy, not tempo.** "A golden Belinda is not tempo
  yet. You need something to go with it." Don't commit to the Belinda line
  unless you already have (or can immediately find) a scaling unit to pair with
  her — otherwise you die to the tempo gap.
- **Most players should take Ruiner instead.** "For a lot of other players, you
  just take the ruiner because it's so much safer." Ruiner is the low-risk
  commit; Belinda is the high-ceiling, high-skill commit.
- **The trinket decides it.** Wand of Divination + gems (Belinda) is the economy
  engine that makes the Belinda line work. Without a gem/trinket economy, lean
  Ruiner.

## Fallback lines

- **Felboar / demons.** Cast the same spells on a golden 4-drop demon (Felboar)
  to scale the shop, then eat a huge shop. Ceiling is enormous ("every shop
  would be 2400"), but: "it's all about do you survive the transition." Only
  pivot if you can survive the turn you spend transitioning.
- **Groundbreaker.** With golden Belinda every spell is ×3, so Groundbreaker can
  reach 20k+ stats. It's the greedier scaling ceiling if you hit it early.

## Traps / anti-patterns

- **Brann is a trap once you have infinite gold.** "As good as Bran is… you
  don't really need rerolls if you have infinite gold." Brann's extra
  gems/shields/rerolls are redundant when Belinda already refunds everything.
- **Freezing / over-thinking in APM.** "It's so easy to get overwhelmed… fog of
  war type stuff." The comp is APM-heavy; the failure mode is stalling, not
  misplaying a single card. Keep moving.
- **Casting on the wrong target.** Ruiner/Fauna buffs require the spell to land
  on a **Naga** (or the amalgam). Casting on a neutral (e.g. Brann) wastes the
  buff.

## Spell-power scaling (the real engine)

Once you have **spell power** (Felfire Conjurer), the engine is **Fauna + Drakkari
+ Balinda**, and **Ruiner falls off** — it doesn't scale with spell power:

> "Ruiner doesn't increase the scaling… it's plus two plus three per spell cast.
> You just want more fauna, more drakari, more Belinda cuz you have spell power."

- **Balinda doubles spells** (cast twice; three times when golden) — "with
  Belinda, he will triple cast."
- **Drakkari works with Fauna** — "Dari works with fauna. I'm not selling that."
- **The T-set amalgam (tier 6) is the best Fauna target** once you have spell
  power — it takes advantage of spell power, so it beats Ruiner as the payoff.
- **More engine pieces, not more Ruiners:** the cards you're rolling for are
  more Fauna, more Drakkari, more Balinda — not Ruiner.

## Scaling ceiling

- Ruiner: fast but "falls off later" — it's the mid-game stabilizer, not the
  end-game cap. Once you have spell power, it's outclassed by Fauna + the T-set
  amalgam.
- Groundbreaker: 20k+ with golden Belinda (every spell ×3).
- Felboar/demons: the true end-game cap if you survive the transition.

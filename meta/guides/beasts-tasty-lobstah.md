# Beasts — Tasty Lobstah

> Extracted from Shadybunny's commentary on Zorgo's game
> (video `hzvWDqhFHok`). Card names normalized from auto-captions.

## The engine

The comp is built around **Tasty Lobster** (summon a Beast → buff it), amplified
by **Titus** (double deathrattles) and **Death Strider** (trigger the leftmost
deathrattle when a Rally unit attacks). **Poet** makes the buffs permanent.

1. **Tasty Lobster** — the core. Summon a Beast → buff it. Reborn it so it
   triggers twice.
2. **Titus** — doubles deathrattles.
3. **Death Strider** — "whenever a rally unit attacks, the death strider will
   trigger the leftmost death rattle."
4. **Rally units** — attack to trigger deathrattles (Al'Akir gives them
   Windfury).
5. **Poet** — makes buffs permanent. "You're not only making the buffs permanent,
   you're also doubling them cuz of poet."
6. **Hyena** — the payoff (scales with Beasts).

## The loop

- Rally units attack → Death Strider triggers the leftmost deathrattle (Lobster).
- Lobster buffs the summoned Beasts; Titus doubles it.
- Poet makes the buffs permanent (especially on an all-type Titus).
- "You can have a loop like that where the fish triggers the griffin which gives
  you another fish which then triggers the griffin" — a self-sustaining trigger
  chain.

## Traps / anti-patterns

- **Keep two Lobsters on the board** so you have something to trigger your
  rallies (they Reborn with Taunt, so Windfury can kill them before you get
  value).
- **Hand-space economy** — the comp generates a lot of cards; manage hand space
  or you overflow and miss Golden Touch value.
- **Dark Gifts can whiff** — don't press the button late for a filler; use it
  when you have no better way to spend gold.

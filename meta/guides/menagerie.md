# Menagerie

> Our advice for the published S-tier build (hsreplay tier list, 2026-09-30).
> Written as original advice for this coach; card texts read from the DB.

## The engine

A **spell-target multiplier engine** built on an all-tribe chassis, so every
spell you cast lands twice and every tribe row stays live.

1. **Gatekeeper Amalgam** (t6, All) — whenever you cast a spell on it, it
   casts Misplaced Tea Set. The engine: a spell becomes two spells.
2. **Balinda Stonehearth** (t6) — your spells that target friendly minions
   cast twice. Aim them at the Amalgam: every cast becomes four.
3. **Brann Bronzebeard** (t5) — battlecries twice, for the zoo of tribed
   bodies around the engine.
4. **Addons lean into the spell economy**: Felfire Conjurer (spells give an
   extra +1/+1 permanently), Felboar (3 spells → consume a Tavern minion for
   its stats), Hot-Air Surveyor (hand Blood Gems cast twice), Victorious
   Geomant (Activate: Blood Gems), Magicfin Mycologist (each spell buy prints
   a 1/1 Murloc that knows it), Dark Puppeteer (deathrattle: permanent spell
   health), Enterprising Escapee (spend-gold Lockboxes).

## The loop

- Cast a targeted spell at Gatekeeper Amalgam → Amalgam casts Tea Set →
  Balinda doubled the original → Felboar's consume counter and Conjurer's
  scaling tick off the same casts.
- Buy spells every turn even off-plan: half this build eats spell CASTS as
  fuel, not just their effects.

## Traps / anti-patterns

- **The Amalgam is the engine, not a body** — it's usually not your biggest
  minion and that's fine; selling it kills the loop.
- **Menagerie is deliberately multi-tribe** — the coach won't filter it by
  the family ban; judge it by whether its core pieces are buyable.
- **Watch the spell TARGETS** — Balinda only doubles friendly-targeted
  spells; AoE/buff-everything spells don't trigger her.

# Aberrations — Deathrattle/Spells

> Our advice for the published S-tier build (hsreplay tier list, 2026-09-26).
> Written as original advice for this coach; card texts read from the DB.

## The engine

A **deathrattle value engine** carried by **Rally** bodies and tavern-spell
generation. Every core card replaces itself or doubles a trigger, so the
board never stops producing.

1. **Headhunter Gryphon** (t4, Beast) — Rally: get a random Beast. Free
   board-wide presence every combat.
2. **Unwilling Slacker** (t2) — deathrattle: get a random 1-Cost tavern
   spell. The early game's spell generator (spell casts feed the Deity).
3. **Titus Rivendare** (t5) — deathrattles trigger twice: double Slacker
   spells, double Converter Deity banks.
4. **Faceless Converter** (t5) — deathrattle: Deity +1/+1, improved by each
   tavern spell you've cast this game.
5. **Deathstrider** (t6, Beast) — after a friendly Rally minion attacks,
   trigger your left-most deathrattle. The reason the Beast bodies and the
   deathrattle package are one build.
6. **Addons**: N'raqi Sapper (Chambers), The Shadow of Doubt (Deity +3/+4 on
   every hand-add), Nightmare Corroder (Sludge Corrosions), Sky Admiral
   Rogers / Proud Privateer (Bounty generation — Bounties cast twice), and
   Sin'dorei Straight Shot (Divine Shield/Windfury tempo + Rally strip).

## The loop

- Rally minions attack each combat → Deathstrider re-fires your left-most
  deathrattle → Slacker prints spells, Converter banks the Deity.
- Spells you cast improve Converter's bank and (with Sapper/Corroder) feed
  the Deity directly.
- The Deity's three-sacrifice meter climbs on every Aberration death — this
  build dies on purpose, often.

## Traps / anti-patterns

- **Deathstrider wants attack ORDER** — the left-most deathrattle is what
  re-fires; position the one you want doubled left-most.
- **Watch the tribe mix** — Gryphon/Deathstrider are Beasts, the payoffs are
  Aberrations, the addons bring Pirates. The build is a deathrattle engine
  that happens to span tribes; don't chase a pure-tribe board.
- **This build and Deity Feed share half a core** (Converter/Titus/Sapper/
  Corroder/Shadow) — committing to one doesn't mean selling the other's
  pieces; they diverge at Deathstrider/Gryphon versus Drakkari/K'Thir.

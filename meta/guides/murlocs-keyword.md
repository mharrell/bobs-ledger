# Murlocs — Keyword

> Our advice for the published S-tier build (hsreplay tier list, 2026-09-26).
> Written as original advice for this coach; card texts read from the DB.

## The engine

A **Bonus Keyword snowball**: murlocs that hand out random keywords, and
payoffs that count them.

1. **Bubble Gunner** (t1) — battlecry: gain a random Bonus Keyword. The
   on-ramp; every murloc body is a keyword carrier from turn 1.
2. **Sewer Escapee** (t4) — Activate (1): give another murloc +7/+7 and a
   random Bonus Keyword. The single biggest per-turn pump in the build.
3. **Hackerfin** (t5) — battlecry: give your OTHER minions +3/+2, improved by
   each different Bonus Keyword in your warband. The payoff for diversifying.
4. **Brann Bronzebeard** (t5) — battlecries trigger twice: two Sewer pumps,
   two Hackerfin sweeps.
5. **Kelp Keeper** (t4) — Activate (1): trigger a friendly minion's
   battlecry — a third Brann in practice.
6. **Young Murk-Eye** (t6) — end of turn: trigger an adjacent minion's
   battlecry. The late-game echo.

## The loop

- Bubble Gunner / Sewer Escapee spread **different** keywords → Hackerfin's
  battlecry grows per distinct keyword → Brann/Kelp Keeper/Murk-Eye multiply
  every battlecry that lands.
- **Bile Spitter** (t5) is the protection: Rally — give another friendly
  murloc Venomous. One poison answer buys the scaling time.
- **Expert Aviator** (t2) — Rally: summon the highest-Attack minion from your
  hand for the combat — an early tempo bridge while the engine comes online.

## Traps / anti-patterns

- **Distinct keywords are the resource.** Duplicated keywords do nothing for
  Hackerfin; Sewer Escapee's random keyword is a feature, not noise.
- **Brann ordering**: activate Kelp Keeper / play murlocs AFTER Brann lands —
  a battlecry that fired before Brann is half a battlecry.
- **Gormling Gourmet (t4)** is the sustain piece (taunt + Seafood Stew on
  both battlecry and deathrattle) — fine to hold, wrong to prioritize over
  the keyword payoffs.
- **Activate budget**: Sewer Escapee and Kelp Keeper each cost 1 gold to
  activate — with Brann up, that 1 gold is buying two pumps. Plan the turn's
  gold around the activations, not just the buys.

# Murlocs — APM

> Extracted from Shadybunny's commentary on Zorgo's game
> (video `0sRiOxp9wVE`). Card names normalized from auto-captions.

## The engine

The comp is an **APM economy loop**: buy 2-drops, sell them, and generate gold +
Murlocs to keep spamming. **Golden River Skipper** is the economy engine, and
**Painter** is the payoff.

1. **Golden River Skipper** — the economy engine. "Once you have golden Skipper,
   Painter is the nuts cuz it procs on everything you do." Buying a Murloc gains
   gold.
2. **Painter** — the payoff. The tier-4 Murloc that AoE-buffs your board if it's
   Murlocs. "Per two drop he sells, he's getting four one drops in return."
3. **Tad** — generates Murlocs.
4. **The APM loop** — buy 2-drops, sell them, generate gold + Murlocs, spam.
   "As long as he doesn't play in a way that leaves him zero gold to roll a
   two-drop, he's infinite."

## The APM loop

- Golden Skipper makes buying Murlocs generate gold.
- Buy 2-drops, sell them, get 1-drops back (via Painter), repeat.
- "He will run out of time before he runs out of money" — the comp is APM-heavy;
  the failure mode is stalling, not misplaying.

## Traps / anti-patterns

- **The spell-power Murloc gimmick doesn't quite work.** "Feels like they
  designed that spell power murloc comp and it doesn't really work that well" —
  you need Rylak + the tier-4 spell generator + Brann, and your hand fills up so
  you can't cycle. Don't force it.
- **The stats aren't worth the carpal tunnel** — the comp is high-effort,
  moderate-yield. It's stable (Painter AoE-buffs) but not a huge ceiling.
- **End game is usually scam** — with money but no huge scaling, buy Bile
  Spitters / scam to close.

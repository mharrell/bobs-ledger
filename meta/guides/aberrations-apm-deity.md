# Aberrations — APM Deity

> Our advice for the published A-tier build (hsreplay tier list, 2026-09-30).
> Written as original advice for this coach; card texts read from the DB.

## The engine

A **spend-gold engine** where every coin spent prints Aberration value:
Bounties, hand-adds, and tavern-spell casts all feed the Deity.

1. **Sky Admiral Rogers** (t6) — after you spend 9 gold, get a random Bounty.
   The metronome: an APM turn of buys/rolls/activates charges it repeatedly.
2. **Proud Privateer** (t5) — your Bounties cast twice.
3. **Sha of Fear** (t7, event-tier) — whenever you cast a tavern spell, give
   your minions AND Deity +4/+3. Every spell is board and bank at once.
4. **The Shadow of Doubt** (t6) — whenever a card is added to your hand, give
   your Deity +3/+4. Bounties and generated cards are hand-adds.
5. **Addons**: Faceless Converter (spell-improved Deity feed), Brann
   (battlecries twice), Enterprising Escapee (more spend-gold Lockboxes).

## The loop

- Spend gold fast (buys, rolls, Activates) → Rogers mints Bounties →
  Privateer casts each twice → spells cast feed Sha of Fear's board+Deity
  pump; Bounty/generator cards entering your hand feed Shadow of Doubt.
- The Deity's 3-sacrifice meter climbs as your Aberrations die; the banked
  pool pays out in combat.

## Traps / anti-patterns

- **Gold in hand is wasted engine fuel** — this build wants your gold SPENT
  each turn; hoarding for a tier is anti-synergy.
- **Sha of Fear is event-tier (t7)** — it will not simply appear in a normal
  shop; plan around it via discovers/generators, not the hunt list.
- **APM order matters**: Brann before battlecry pieces, Privateer before the
  Bounty spam starts.

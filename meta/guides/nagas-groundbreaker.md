# Nagas — Groundbreaker

> Extracted from Shadybunny's commentary on a Marin game
> (video `fUZ07XMcvfo`). Card names normalized from auto-captions.

## The engine

The comp is built around **Groundbreaker** (play a Naga → gain stats, improved
by every 3 spells you've cast). **Seafloor Recruiter** generates spells, and
**spell power** + **Belinda** amplify them.

1. **Groundbreaker** — the payoff. "After you play a Naga, gain +1/+1. (Improved
   by every 3 spells you've cast this game!)"
2. **Seafloor Recruiter** — generates spells.
3. **Spell power** — boosts the spells.
4. **Belinda** — doubles spells. "Belinda gives you more spell triggers which
   grows your ground breakers."

## The loop

- Cast spells (Seafloor Recruiter generates them) → Groundbreaker scales.
- Spell power boosts the spells; Belinda doubles them.
- "It's really about ground breakers and then add some scam."

## Traps / anti-patterns

- **Groundbreaker is the win condition** — roll for it, don't miss it.
- **Belinda is worth keeping** for the extra spell triggers (it grows the
  Groundbreakers).
- **Add scam to close** — the comp scales stats but needs scam (Surf n' Surf,
  blaster) to beat big boards.

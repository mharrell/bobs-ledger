# Elementals — Stat Scaling

> Extracted from Shadybunny's commentary on Beater's game
> (video `og2NYl_Ri7U`). Card names normalized from auto-captions.

## The engine

The comp is built around **Nomi** (play an Elemental → buff Elementals in the
Tavern) feeding **Unbound Tempest** (play an Elemental → gain stats). **Mana
Surge** and **Glowing Cinder** add more scaling.

1. **Nomi** — the core. "After you play an Elemental, give Elementals in the
   Tavern +4/+4 this game." Note: Nomi is NOT an Elemental, so it doesn't buff
   itself — it buffs the shop.
2. **Unbound Tempest** — the payoff. "The Unbound scales better than the mana
   surge does if you have a lot of stats in the shop."
3. **Mana Surge** — scales with Elementals.
4. **Glowing Cinder** — buffs Elementals.
5. **Brann** — doubles battlecries.

## The loop

- Play Elementals → Nomi buffs the shop → Unbound Tempest scales.
- "He just needs to make sure that when he plays the elementals, he has an
  elemental in the shop" — otherwise Unbound gets no value.
- Golden Nomi + Golden Brann + Golden Drakkari is the high-roll.

## Traps / anti-patterns

- **Don't board-lock yourself** — "I've done that where you're like, 'Oh, I'll
  just play this elemental first and oh, I've board locked myself and I don't
  have gold now to buy it.'"
- **Unbound needs an Elemental in the shop** to trigger — play it when the shop
  has one.
- **Nomi is not an Elemental** — it doesn't get the buffs, but it's the engine
  that makes the shop huge.

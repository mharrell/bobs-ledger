Bob's Ledger now runs on macOS — and it stops losing the end of your session.

THE BUG WORTH THE DOWNLOAD

Hearthstone caps Power.log at 10 MB. Once a log reached that cap the rest of the
session went unread, so the coach quietly stopped seeing games — this one was
never macOS-only, it could happen on Windows too. The launcher now removes the
cap, and says so when it checks your setup. If your log had been capped, whole
sessions come back.

ON macOS

* Double-click "Start Bob's Ledger.command". It now ships with its executable
  bit set, so a fresh download runs the first time instead of demanding
  permission.
* Sharing a session and checking for updates both work now. Every https call
  used to fail on a Mac with a certificate error, so neither could ever have
  succeeded.
* The wide three-column layout appears on a laptop: it needs 1650px of window
  rather than 1800, which is what a maximised 16" MacBook actually has.

THE PAGE

* One palette from top to bottom — the tab row and the header are the same brown
  as the panels under them.
* Your hand is a single line of text, not a row of card tiles. You are already
  looking at the real one.
* The tavern is a compact list: a thumbnail, the card, its price and what is
  left in the pool. Five offers cost about two thirds of the height they did.
* Comps opens first and stays open; its sort control sits on the panel's
  heading; comp names are no longer cut off, and how many copies you own sits
  beside the name instead of at the far edge of the row.
* New comp: Dragons - Golden Tarecgosa, with the two trinkets that turn a
  two-minion board into a problem.

The README is rewritten for someone who has just downloaded it, with a Windows
flow and a macOS flow. Screenshots come back in a later release.

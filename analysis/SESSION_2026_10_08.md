## Where the 2026-10-08 session left off (the phantom shop turns — main 32aeecb, RELEASED)


**The replay viewer's "shop" cards that showed gold `-g`, `ENDED WITH —` and
"played 0 cards" were FIGHTS, misread as turns.** Battlegrounds also runs
fights inside their own GameState `MAIN_ACTION..MAIN_END` pair — measured: a
pair 56 ms apart holding 6,947 lines of ATTACK/DEATHS blocks and never a shop
offer. The turn counter took that `MAIN_ACTION` for a shop opening, so every
such fight became a phantom turn: its drain snapshots stamped phase="buy"
into a phantom bucket ("OPENED WITH" was the survivors mid-drain, which
passes the 7-cap filter), the drained-empty instant was "ENDED WITH —", and
no offers meant `analyze()` never ran (gold/actions empty). One evening game
carried FOUR; round 1's fight was a phantom in all five saved replays
checked; every later turn number was shifted. Fixed in `live_coach` +
`turn_review` (main `32aeecb`):

1. **A `MAIN_ACTION` is only PENDING; the turn opens when the shop table
   fills** (`_zone_commit` — the first proof of a shop in the log). A pair
   that closes unpromoted was a fight: no turn number, no pairing write, and
   its drain stamps `combat` into the fight's OWN bucket — which is also why
   more fights now have a readable winner (the finals fight read `them`
   where it read `None` before). The remaining `winner=None` rows are the
   night session's open item 1, reduced, not closed.
2. **The promotion is keyed on `actions.pending_buy`, not a coach-level
   flag** — the actions tracker sees a STEP line only at the END of feed();
   a coach-level flag promoted one line early against the previous phase's
   still-buffered offers (the MAIN_ACTION wipe commits them before
   clearing). Found by the scout tests refusing to pass, not by inspection.
3. `turn_review` keeps a row for analyzed phases with no snapshot bucket, so
   a do-nothing shop no longer silently renumbers the game.
4. Verified on the whole 20:06 session: 14 rows for 14 real recruit phases,
   gold and the player's real actions on every row (was 17 rows, 4 fights
   posing as shops). `TestCombatStepPair` is the regression; 8 fixtures that
   fed a bare `MAIN_ACTION` were updated to prove a shop the way the log
   does.

5. **Second fix of the day (main `ac0dd28`, after the `48c1023` tier
   refresh): the played-card snapshot.** Reported with the 08:14 Xavius
   game: turn 1 said "bought and played Dune Dweller" but ENDED WITH read
   "—". Two measured causes, both pre-existing and made visible by the
   phantom-turn fix: (a) the play cascade writes `ZONE_POSITION=0` while
   the card is still tracked HAND, *before* `ZONE=PLAY` — the 09-14
   ghost-guard re-zoned it SETASIDE, so the play read as the ignored
   SETASIDE→PLAY shape; board_state now remembers the hand exit and
   snapshots that play (combat summons and ghosts still don't; the hero's
   pick-UI transit needed a cardtype gate or it fired an empty TURN 0).
   (b) the game writes the new phase's offers BEFORE the `MAIN_ACTION`
   line, and the step wipe discarded that proven table — a no-action shop
   never re-prints it, so the promotion hung on a re-print 1400 lines in;
   a table filled since the last MAIN_END is now proof at the step itself.
   Verified on the reported game (T1 ENDED WITH reads Dune Dweller 3/3, no
   TURN 0) and the evening regression game (14/14). NOT yet in a release;
   the 08:14 replay keeps its old shape until one is cut and re-saved.
